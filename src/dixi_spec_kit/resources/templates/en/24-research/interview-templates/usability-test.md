# Usability test guide

## Purpose

Give facilitators a reusable script to observe people attempting real tasks with a prototype or the product, so that usability and accessibility problems are found before or after release with evidence, in each supported language.

## Script

### Introduction

> We are testing the product, not you. Please think aloud as you go: tell me what you are looking for, what you expect and what surprises you. If you get stuck, that is useful for us. May we record the screen and audio?

### For each task

1. Read the scenario aloud and give it to the participant in writing.
2. Observe without helping; if asked, reply "What would you do if I weren't here?"
3. Note path, hesitations, errors, comments and whether the criterion was met.
4. After the task ask: "How easy or difficult was that, from 1 to 5? Why?"

### Wrap-up

- What was the most confusing part?
- What, if anything, would you change?

## Severity scale

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Critical | Prevents task completion |
| Serious | Causes significant delay or frustration |
| Minor | Small annoyance; task still completed easily |

Accessibility barriers that block a task are rated Critical.

## Reporting

- List each problem with its severity, how many participants hit it and supporting evidence.
- File the report in the research log and link problems to feature specs or defects.
