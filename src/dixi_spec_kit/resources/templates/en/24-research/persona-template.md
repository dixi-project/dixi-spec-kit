# Persona template

## Purpose

Provide a format for describing a representative user, based on research evidence, so that specs, designs and priorities refer to real needs instead of to an imagined "average user". Copy the template section once per persona.

Status: draft. {{pending: list the personas the project needs and the research each one comes from}}

## Rules

- A persona summarizes patterns seen in several participants; it is not one real person and contains no personal data.
- Every statement points to evidence; mark guesses as assumptions until validated.
- Keep between two and five personas; more usually means the audience is unclear.
- Describe behaviour and goals, not demographics alone.
- Consider language, region, devices, connectivity and accessibility needs.

## Template

```markdown
### <Persona name> — <short role description>

**Evidence**: <studies or data this persona is based on, with dates>
**Confidence**: <assumed | partly validated | validated>

**Context**
- Role and situation: <...>
- Languages used: <...>
- Devices and connectivity: <...>
- Accessibility needs: <for example uses screen magnification>

**Goals**
- <what they want to achieve>

**Frustrations**
- <what gets in the way today>

**Current behaviour**
- <how they solve the problem now>

**What would make them switch or stay**
- <...>

**Representative quote** (anonymized)
> "<...>"

**Implications for the product**
- <decisions this persona should influence>
```

- Record what changed and why.
