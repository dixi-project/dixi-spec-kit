# Research log

## Purpose

Keep a dated, searchable record of every research activity and its findings, so that evidence behind decisions can be found later, studies are not repeated by accident and knowledge survives when people leave.

Status: draft. {{pending: decide where raw research data is stored and who can access it}}

## What goes here

- One entry file per study or research activity, named `YYYY-MM-DD-short-topic.md`.
- Summaries and anonymized findings only.
- Raw recordings, transcripts and personal data stay in the access-controlled storage chosen for research, never in this repository.

## Entry template

```markdown
# RS-<nnn> — <topic>

- Date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
- Owner: <role>
- Plan: <link to the research plan>
- Method: <interviews, usability test, survey, analytics review>
- Participants: <number and segments, no names>
- Languages and markets: <...>

## Questions
- <research questions>

## Findings
| Finding | Evidence (how many participants, data) | Confidence |
|---------|----------------------------------------|------------|
| <...> | <...> | <low, medium, high> |

## Recommendations
- <action> → <roadmap item, spec or decision>

## Open questions
- <what remains unknown>
```

## Rules

- Write the entry within a week of the study ending.
- Distinguish observations from interpretations; state confidence honestly.
- Never include names, contact details or identifiable quotes.
- Do not edit past findings to fit later conclusions; add a new entry instead.
- Inconclusive and negative results are logged too.
