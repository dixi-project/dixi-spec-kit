## Consent and data handling

- Participants receive a plain-language consent form in their language before the session.
- Recordings only with explicit consent; participants can stop at any time.
- Store raw data in an access-controlled location; delete it after {{pending: period}}.
- Share only anonymized findings.

## Analysis

- Take notes per session the same day.
- Cluster observations into themes; count how many participants showed each.
- Separate observations (what happened) from interpretations (what it may mean).

## Outputs

- Findings summary with evidence, filed in the research log.
- Updates to personas or jobs if warranted.
- Recommendations linked to the decision this study informs.
