# Data processing agreement (DPA) outline

> **Working aid, not legal advice.** This outline lists the topics a data processing agreement usually covers so the team can gather facts before counsel drafts or reviews the contract. It is not a contract, and no template certifies compliance.

## Purpose

Prepare, for each processing relationship, the factual annex a DPA needs: who processes what data, why, where, with which safeguards and under which sub-processors. Counsel owns the legal wording.

## Topics the agreement usually addresses

- Processing only on documented instructions of the controller.
- Confidentiality of the people authorised to process the data.
- Technical and organisational security measures: point to [Data protection controls](../13-security/data-protection-controls.md).
- Engagement of sub-processors, notice of changes and right to object: point to [Sub-processors](gdpr/sub-processors.md).
- Assistance with data subject requests and with impact assessments.
- Breach notification to the controller without undue delay: point to [Incident response](../13-security/incident-response.md).
- Deletion or return of data at the end of the service.
- Information and audit rights: {{pending: define the evidence the project can offer}}
