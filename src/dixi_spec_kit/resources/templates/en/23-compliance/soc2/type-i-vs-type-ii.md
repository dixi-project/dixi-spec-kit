# SOC 2 Type I vs Type II

> **Working aid, not legal advice.** This page helps the team record a decision about the report type. It does not predict an audit outcome and no template certifies compliance.

## Purpose

Record which SOC 2 report type the project will aim for first, and why, based on customer demand, maturity of controls and available time and budget.

## Differences in general terms

| Aspect | Type I | Type II |
|--------|--------|---------|
| What is evaluated | Design of controls at a point in time | Design and operating effectiveness over a period |
| Observation period | None (a single date) | A period agreed with the auditor |
| Evidence needed | Controls exist and are designed suitably | Controls operated consistently throughout the period |
| Typical use | First step, early assurance | What many business customers ask for |
| Main risk | May not satisfy customers who need operating evidence | Gaps during the period appear as exceptions |

## Options

1. **Type I first, then Type II**: earlier assurance, two audit cycles.
2. **Type II directly**: one cycle, but controls must be operating from the start of the period.
3. **Defer**: answer questionnaires with existing evidence and revisit at a set date.
