# Sub-processors

> **Working aid, not legal advice.** This register helps keep the list of third parties that process personal data accurate. It does not certify compliance; contractual and transfer questions need qualified counsel.

## Purpose

Keep a current, verifiable list of every third party that processes personal data on the project's behalf, what it processes, where, and under which agreement, so customers and data subjects can be informed and changes can be notified.

Status: draft. {{pending: decide who approves new sub-processors and how customers are notified of changes}}

## Inclusion criteria

A third party belongs in this list when it stores, accesses or otherwise processes personal data from the product. Include hosting, email delivery, support tools, analytics, payment, monitoring and AI model providers when they receive personal data. Tools that never touch personal data stay in the general vendor catalogue.

## Change process

1. Propose the new sub-processor with purpose and data categories.
2. Complete the [Vendor risk assessment](../../15-operations/vendor-management/vendor-risk-assessment.md).
3. Confirm the agreement covers the topics in the [DPA template](../dpa-template.md).
4. Notify customers with the notice period agreed in contracts: {{pending: notice period}}
5. Activate only after the notice period, or after objections are resolved.
6. On retirement, confirm deletion or return of data and keep the row with its end date.
