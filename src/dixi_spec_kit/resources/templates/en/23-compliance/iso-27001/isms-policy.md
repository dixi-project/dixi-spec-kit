# Information security policy (ISMS)

> **Working aid, not legal advice.** This is a skeleton for the top-level information security policy of an ISMS. Adapt it to the organisation and have it approved by management; no template certifies compliance.

## Purpose

State management's commitment to information security, the scope it covers, the objectives it sets and how the policy is kept current. Detailed rules live in the specific standards and procedures it references.

## 2. Commitments

Management commits to:

- protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information in scope;
- meet applicable legal, regulatory and contractual requirements, identified in the [Compliance overview](../compliance-overview.md);
- assess and treat information security risks with a documented method, recorded in the [Risk register](../../13-security/risk-register.md);
- provide the resources and competence needed to run the ISMS;
- continually improve the ISMS.
