# ISO/IEC 27001 readiness

> **Working aid, not legal advice.** This plan organises preparation for an information security management system (ISMS) aligned with ISO/IEC 27001. Only an accredited certification body issues a certificate; no template certifies compliance. Read the standard itself from its official publisher.

## Purpose

Decide whether ISO/IEC 27001 certification is worth pursuing and track the work to build an ISMS that runs on its own: scope, risk process, controls, internal audit and management review.

## Typical path

1. Define scope and policy.
2. Run the risk assessment and choose treatments.
3. Prepare the statement of applicability.
4. Operate controls and collect records for a period: {{pending: decide the period}}
5. Hold an internal audit and a management review.
6. Engage the certification body for the staged audit.
