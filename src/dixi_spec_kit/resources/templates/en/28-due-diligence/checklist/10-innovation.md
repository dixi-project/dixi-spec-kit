# Checklist 10 — Innovation

> **Evidence rule.** Score only what an artifact proves. A missing artifact is recorded as "no evidence" and scored 0; it is never filled with an assumption presented as fact. Claims about differentiation need a source, not adjectives.

## Purpose

Assess whether the product can keep improving: how the team discovers needs, experiments, adopts new technology (including AI) responsibly, and what is genuinely the organisation's own technology.
