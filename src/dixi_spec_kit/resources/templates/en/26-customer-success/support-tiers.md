## Severity

| Severity | Definition | Example |
|----------|------------|---------|
| SEV1 | Service unavailable or data at risk for many users | Nobody can sign in |
| SEV2 | Major feature broken, no reasonable workaround | Payments fail for a market |
| SEV3 | Feature degraded, workaround exists | Export is slow |
| SEV4 | Question, minor issue or request | How to change a setting |

SEV1 and SEV2 follow the incident process in addition to support handling.

## Triage

1. Confirm the request is understood; ask for missing details using a template.
2. Assign severity based on impact, not on how the request is phrased.
3. Search for known issues and help articles before investigating.
4. Route to the right queue or team; record the decision.
5. Keep the customer updated in their language at agreed intervals.
