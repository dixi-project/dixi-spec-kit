# Customer success strategy

## Purpose

Define how the project helps users and customers reach the outcome they came for, how their health is measured and how the team engages with each segment through onboarding, adoption, renewal and expansion. Success means the customer achieves their goal, not that they use more features.

## Customer journey

| Stage | Customer goal | Our responsibility | Signal of success |
|-------|---------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Onboarding | Get set up and see first value | Guided setup, clear first steps | First key action completed |
| Adoption | Use the product in their routine | Help, tips, timely nudges | Regular active use |
| Value | Reach the outcome they wanted | Check progress against their goal | Goal confirmed by the customer |
| Renewal | Decide to continue | Show value delivered | Renewal or continued use |
| Expansion | Use more where it helps | Suggest only when it fits their goal | Growth in use or plan |

## Health score

A health score combines signals into one indicator of risk or success. Keep it simple and explainable.

## Onboarding principles

- Ask for the customer's goal first and tailor the first steps to it.
- Measure time to first value and shorten it.
- Onboarding content exists in every supported language.
