# Customer breach communication

## Purpose

Inform affected people and customers about a data breach accurately, promptly and in plain language, telling them what happened and what they can do.

Status: draft. {{pending: name the communications lead role}}

This is a communication guide, not legal advice. The privacy or legal contact approves every message and decides who must be notified.

## When it applies

After the [data breach](data-breach.md) playbook decides that notification is required or advisable.

## What every message contains

1. What happened, in one or two sentences, and when.
2. Which data was involved, and which was not.
3. What has been done to stop it.
4. What the person can do now (change password, watch for phishing, contact their bank).
5. How to get help: a dedicated contact and, if offered, support services.

## Template

> Subject: Important information about your data
>
> On {{pending: date}} we discovered that {{pending: plain description}}. The information involved was {{pending: data categories}}. {{pending: data not involved, e.g. passwords or payment details}} was not affected.
>
> We have {{pending: containment actions}}. We recommend that you {{pending: concrete steps}}.
>
> If you have questions, contact {{pending: dedicated channel}}. We are sorry this happened.

## Rules

- Be factual; do not minimise or speculate. Say what is still being investigated.
- Never include more personal data in the message than the person already has.
- Do not use links that look like phishing; prefer instructions to go to the product directly.
- Send in the language of each person; prepare every supported language. {{pending: list languages}}
- An AI agent may draft; a person approves and sends.

## Close criteria

- All required audiences notified and the sending recorded.
- Support has a reply macro and an escalation path for questions.
