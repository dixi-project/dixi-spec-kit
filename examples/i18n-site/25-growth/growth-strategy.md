# Growth strategy

## Purpose

Define how the project attracts, activates and keeps its users, which single metric best reflects delivered value, and how growth work is prioritised and reviewed. Growth decisions are product decisions: they follow specs and are measured, not assumed.

## International growth

- Each market and language is measured separately; averages hide problems.
- Content is localised, not literally translated, and served on language-prefixed URLs.
- Prices, examples and legal text are reviewed per market.

## Cadence

- Weekly: review input metrics and running initiatives.
- Monthly: review the funnel by channel and language and reprioritise.
- Quarterly: review the north star definition and channel mix.

## Principles

- Every initiative states a hypothesis, a metric and a stop condition before it starts.
- Personal data used for growth follows the data classification and consent rules.
- No dark patterns: no hidden costs, forced continuity or misleading urgency.
